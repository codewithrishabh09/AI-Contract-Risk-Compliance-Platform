# 🚧 AI Contract Risk & Compliance Platform

> **Status: Under Construction 🚧**

An AI-powered platform designed to analyze legal contracts, identify potential risks, detect compliance issues, and extract important contractual obligations.

## 🚧 Project Status

This project is currently under active development. I am building the platform step by step, focusing on reliable contract analysis, risk identification, compliance monitoring, and an intuitive user experience.

### 🛠️ Planned Features

* 📄 **Contract Analysis** — Upload and analyze legal documents.
* ⚠️ **Risk Detection** — Identify potentially risky clauses and contractual terms.
* ⚖️ **Compliance Analysis** — Highlight potential compliance gaps.
* 🔍 **Clause Extraction** — Extract important clauses, obligations, and deadlines.
* 🤖 **AI-Powered Insights** — Generate summaries and contextual explanations.
* 📊 **Risk Assessment** — Categorize contract risks based on their severity.
* 🔐 **Secure Document Handling** — Implement appropriate document security and access controls.
